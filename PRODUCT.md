# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bridal and evening wear shoppers — primarily women in the UAE and Gulf region aged 22–45, planning weddings, galas, or formal events. Includes brides-to-be, bridal parties, and high-net-worth individuals seeking bespoke couture. Context: aspirational, research-heavy, emotionally charged purchase decisions. Also includes partners and family members involved in the selection process.

## Product Purpose

Atelier Riman is Sharjah's premier bridal and evening couture house. It exists to provide exquisitely crafted gowns and formal wear that blend heritage craftsmanship with modern sophistication. Success is measured by the confidence and joy clients feel in their garments, the atelier's reputation for uncompromising quality, and its position as the definitive luxury fashion destination in the region.

## Brand Personality

Regal, Meticulous, Intimate. The voice is poetic yet precise — every word earns its place. Confident in its heritage but never aloof. Speaks to the client as a collaborator in creating something extraordinary. Warmth and approachability underpin the formality.

## Anti-references

Cheap/fast fashion aesthetics. No loud, trendy, or disposable-fashion visual language. Avoid anything that reads as mass-produced or off-the-rack. The brand should never feel like it's trying too hard to be current.

## Design Principles

1. **Craft over flash.** Every visual element should feel deliberate and hand-considered. Ornament serves structure, not the other way around.
2. **Warm grandeur.** Opulence without coldness. Gold and dark tones should feel inviting, not austere.
3. **Heritage made present.** Traditional elegance expressed through modern typography, motion, and interaction patterns. Never costumed.
4. **Intimacy at scale.** The site should feel personal and consultative even as it showcases a global-ready brand.
5. **Every detail matters.** Spacing, rhythm, micro-interactions, and copy tone all carry the brand's meticulous ethos.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Sufficient color contrast on all text. Keyboard-navigable interfaces. Focus states on interactive elements. Semantic HTML structure.
